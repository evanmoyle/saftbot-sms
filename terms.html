Program

SaftBot is an optional SMS program operated by Evan Moyle. It sends informational tips about roster and schedule decisions for a private recreational sports league among friends.

SaftBot messages are informational only and do not offer or promote any paid contests, prizes, or money transactions.

SMS sender: +1 (218) 501-5984.

Consent & opt-in

Participation is voluntary. There is no purchase required. Consent is for SaftBot SMS only.

  • Opt-in is by text message only (no web form). Text START to +1 (218) 501-5984.
  • You receive a text asking you to reply YES to confirm. Recurring messages begin only after you reply YES.
  • After YES, you receive a confirmation text acknowledging enrollment.
  • By texting START, you agree to receive recurring informational SMS text messages from SaftBot (Evan Moyle). Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Only people who have given that consent are messaged.

Message frequency & rates

Up to 4 messages per week during the season. Fewer in the offseason.

Message and data rates may apply.

Help & opt-out

Reply STOP to cancel anytime (honored immediately). Reply HELP for help.
