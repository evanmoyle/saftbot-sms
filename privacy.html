SaftBot Privacy Policy

Effective: September 17, 2026. Operated by Evan Moyle (sole proprietor). Program name: SaftBot.

Information we collect

We collect mobile phone numbers and names of people who opt in to SaftBot SMS, plus message content needed to send and receive those texts.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used only to send and receive SaftBot SMS: optional informational tips about roster and schedule decisions for a private recreational sports league among friends. Messages are not used for gambling, betting, or wagering.

Message frequency: up to 4 messages per week during the season; fewer in the offseason.

Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

Mobile information / phone numbers will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell phone numbers or SMS opt-in records. Numbers may be processed by Twilio solely to deliver and receive SMS.

Retention

We keep opt-in records and numbers while you remain opted in, and for a reasonable period after opt-out for compliance.
