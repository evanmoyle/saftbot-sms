SaftBot Privacy Policy

Effective: September 26, 2026. Operated by Evan Moyle (sole proprietor). Program name: SaftBot.

Information we collect

We collect mobile phone numbers of people who opt in to SaftBot SMS by texting START to +1 (218) 501-5984 and replying YES to confirm, plus message content needed to send and receive those texts. There is no web form; opt-in is by text message only.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used only to send and receive SaftBot SMS: optional informational tips about roster and schedule decisions for a private recreational sports league among friends. Messages are informational only.

Message frequency: up to 4 messages per week during the season; fewer in the offseason.

Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

Mobile information / phone numbers will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell phone numbers or SMS opt-in records. Numbers may be processed by Twilio solely to deliver and receive SMS.

Opt out & help

Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help.

Retention

We keep opt-in records and numbers while you remain opted in, and for a reasonable period after opt-out for compliance.
